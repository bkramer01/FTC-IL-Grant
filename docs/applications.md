# Applications

> **Address:** `/applications` · **Last updated:** October 19, 2026

One row per grant form response, newest first. This is where the Grant Administrator reviews each application.

## Finding applications

- Search by team number, team name or coach.
- **Show** filter: All, Not yet decided, Approved, Not approved, Duplicate submissions, Title 1 status needed.
- The line under the heading counts applications, teams and teams that submitted more than once.

## Flags

- **duplicate ×2**: the team sent the form more than once. Both rows stay.
- **not in BioBuzz**: the team number isn't on the BioBuzz list, so there's no team name.
- **form says …**: the team age the coach picked on the form doesn't match the team number.
- **Title 1 status needed**: the category can't be worked out until School Title 1 Status is entered.

## Columns

| Column | Source |
|---|---|
| Application Timestamp, Coach 1 (+ email), Rookie/Veteran Status Provided, List of Volunteers | Form |
| Team Name | BioBuzz, by team number |
| Calculated Team Age, Rookie/Veteran Status for Grant | Calculated |
| Fully Registered? | Form ("Team is in the green") |
| End of Season Survey Completed? | Form question 2 ("rookie Biobuzz" shows as N/A) |
| School Title 1 Status, Matching Grant Letter Received?, Event Volunteering?, Qualified Purchase?, VRS 6 Lesson Challenge Completed?, Team Approved for Grant, Approval Notes | Grant Administrator (yellow) |
| Additional Info Email Sent | Latest "Additional Info Needed" entry in the Email Log |

The **Letter ↗** and **Screenshot ↗** links open the files the coach uploaded with the form.
