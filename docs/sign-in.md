# Sign In

> **Address:** `/login` · **Last updated:** October 19, 2026

The whole site requires a login. Anyone not signed in is sent here.

- One shared account: username **FTCAdmin**, password from the `ADMIN_PASSWORD` setting on the server.
- After 5 wrong passwords from the same address, sign-in is blocked for 15 minutes.
- Sessions last 12 hours. **Sign out** is at the right end of the blue menu bar.
