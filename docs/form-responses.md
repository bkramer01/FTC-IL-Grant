# Form Responses

> **Address:** `/responses` · **Last updated:** October 22, 2026

A read-only copy of the grant form's Google Sheet ("Form Responses 1"), as of the last **Refresh Data** on the Dashboard ("not refreshed yet" before the first one).

- Every form column is shown with a short heading; hover a heading for the full question. All 19 columns of the sheet are shown, including **City State** (column P) and **Zip** (column S, added to the form Oct 2026). Responses sent before Zip was added have a blank Zip.
- The table scrolls inside its own box, no taller than the window, so the headings stay frozen at the top as you scroll down. Scroll sideways with the scrollbar just under the headings or the one at the bottom of the box.
- Uploaded files (matching grant letter, VRS screenshot) show as **Open file ↗** links.
- The card title shows the number of responses. The line under the page title shows when the copy was taken (Central time).
- Nothing here can be edited. Review entries belong on Applications. To change an answer, edit the Google Sheet and click **Refresh Data**.
- Which columns the rest of the site uses, and how, is listed in [Data and Refresh Data](data-and-refresh.md#form-columns-the-site-uses).
