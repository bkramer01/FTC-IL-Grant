# Email Log

> **Address:** `/emails` · **Last updated:** October 19, 2026

A list of the grant emails sent to coaches. Emails are written and sent outside the site; this page records them. Sending them automatically could come later.

- **Log an email:** date (defaults to today), team (picking one fills in the coach's name), recipient, purpose (Add'l Info Needed, Grant Award, Other) and notes. Notes is a five-line box with no length limit (longer notes scroll, and the box can be dragged taller); line breaks show in the table. "Add'l Info Needed" is a short label used in the dropdown and the Emails sent table; the entry is saved as "Additional Info Needed".
- **Emails sent:** newest first, with team name. **Delete** removes an entry.
- "Additional Info Needed" entries fill in **Add'l Info Email Sent** on Applications. "Grant Award" entries fill in **Grant Award Email Sent** on Fundraising Status.
