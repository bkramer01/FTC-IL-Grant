# Email Log

> **Address:** `/emails` · **Last updated:** October 23, 2026

A list of the grant emails sent to coaches. Emails are written and sent outside the site; this page records them. Sending them automatically could come later.

- **Log an email:** date (defaults to today, Central time), team, recipient, purpose (Add'l Info Needed, Grant Award, Other) and notes. Notes is a five-line box with no length limit (longer notes scroll, and the box can be dragged taller); line breaks show in the table. "Add'l Info Needed" is a short label used in the dropdown and the Emails sent table; the entry is saved as "Additional Info Needed".
  - **Team** lists every team that has sent the grant form, by team number and name. A response whose Team Number isn't a single number (flagged **not one team #** on Applications) isn't listed until it's fixed in the sheet. Picking one fills in **Coach name / recipient** with the coach from the form; it can be changed.
  - Date, team and recipient are required. **Add to log** saves the entry right away.
- **Emails sent (N):** every logged email, newest date first, with team number, team name, recipient, purpose and notes. **Delete** removes an entry immediately (there's no undo).
- "Additional Info Needed" entries fill in **Add'l Info Email Sent** on Applications. "Grant Award" entries fill in **Grant Award Email Sent** on the Dashboard's Check Status tab. When a team has several, the latest date is shown. Deleting an entry updates those columns. See [Site Structure](site-structure.md#how-the-pages-connect).
